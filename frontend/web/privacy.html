2. Scope

This privacy policy applies to the UniBrief website, mobile applications and support communications.

3. Personal data we process

  • Name and email address.
  • Passwords stored as cryptographic hashes.
  • Authentication and refresh tokens.
  • User IDs and project information.
  • Uploaded PDF files and file metadata.
  • Extracted requirements and checklists.

  • Translation content and cached translations.
  • AI usage counters and security records.
  • IP addresses and technical request information.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the requested service: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Compliance with legal obligations: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Consent, where specifically requested: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Our legitimate interests include protecting accounts, maintaining service availability and preventing misuse.

5. Artificial intelligence and OpenAI

UniBrief uses the OpenAI API for requirement extraction, submission analysis and translation. Relevant extracted PDF text, requirements and translation inputs may be transmitted to OpenAI.

We do not use your content to train our own AI models. OpenAI's processing and retention of API data are subject to the applicable API terms and data controls.

Further information: OpenAI API data controls .

Please do not upload sensitive personal data, confidential documents or personal data of other individuals without a lawful basis. AI-generated results may be inaccurate and should be reviewed independently.

6. External service providers

  • OpenAI: AI processing and translation.
  • Cloudflare R2: Storage of uploaded PDF files.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Website hosting and delivery.
  • Render: Backend and database infrastructure.
  • Google Gmail: Support communications and deletion requests.

7. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where applicable, international transfers require safeguards under Articles 44–49 GDPR.

Information about applicable transfer safeguards can be requested using our contact email.

8. Data retention

  • Account information is retained while the account remains active, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Projects, PDFs and analysis results are retained until the relevant content or account is deleted.
  • Translation cache entries associated with a project or account are included in the corresponding deletion process.
  • Account-linked AI usage records and refresh tokens are included in account deletion.
  • Failed-login records may remain for security purposes until they are cleared or removed by the cleanup process.
  • Support messages are retained as necessary to handle requests and legal obligations.
  • Provider logs, backups and AI processing records may have separate retention periods under the applicable provider arrangements.

9. Account and data deletion

You can delete individual PDFs and projects within UniBrief. You can also request permanent account deletion through the application or our public account deletion page.

Account deletion covers account information, projects, registered uploaded PDFs, requirements, checklists, submission results, associated translation cache entries, refresh tokens and account-linked AI usage records.

Certain security records, legally required records and provider backups may remain where necessary. Deletion from UniBrief does not necessarily result in immediate deletion from all external provider systems.

11. Cookies and local storage

UniBrief uses local storage or device storage for authentication and functional preferences. This page stores your selected language locally. We do not intentionally use advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

12. Children

UniBrief is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 16. Please contact us if a child has provided personal data inappropriately.

معلومات OpenAI حول معالجة البيانات

يرجى عدم رفع بيانات شخصية حساسة أو وثائق سرية أو بيانات أشخاص آخرين دون أساس قانوني مناسب. قد تحتوي نتائج الذكاء الاصطناعي على أخطاء، ولذلك يجب مراجعتها بشكل مستقل.

  • قد تخضع سجلات المزودين والنسخ الاحتياطية وبيانات معالجة الذكاء الاصطناعي لفترات احتفاظ منفصلة.
